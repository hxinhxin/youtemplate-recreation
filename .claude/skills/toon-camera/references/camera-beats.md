## Distance discipline

Keep the camera-to-character distance roughly constant across a lateral dash
(here ~6.6 m throughout). A dash *toward* the camera changes subject size by 2×
in three frames, which reads as a zoom error rather than speed. If the action
must come at the camera, cut the travel distance to under a third and let the
lens do the work.

## Framing heights

Aim at chest height (~1.10 m on a 2.18 m cartoon figure), not head height. The
big cartoon head then sits in the upper third naturally and the feet stay in
frame through a crouch.
