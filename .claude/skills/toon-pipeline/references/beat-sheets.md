# Beat Sheets

Frames are at 24 fps. A "punchy loop" lives between 48 and 96 frames (2–4 s).

## Snap dash (the reference shot, `shots.snap_demo`)

| Frames | Beat | What sells it |
|--------|------|---------------|
| 1–10 | Idle | one slow breath, so the wind-up has something to break |
| 10–16 | Anticipation | crouch + counter-lean + arms cocked; ease *out* of idle |
| 16–18 | Hitch | dead hold on the wind-up — the eye locks on |
| 18–21 | Explosive move | 3 frames only, stretched along travel, limbs trailing |
| 21–23 | Abrupt stop | quintic deceleration, wide squash on the contact frame |
| 23–27 | Overshoot / recoil | blow past the landing pose, then rebound |
| 27–31 | Settle | decaying wobble, amplitude × 0.45 each swing |
| 31–56 | Return to idle | snap turn back to camera, breathe |
| 56–72 | Idle + blink | close the loop |

## Other useful budgets

| Move | Anticipation | Action | Stop | Settle |
|------|--------------|--------|------|--------|
| Head snap | 3 f | 2 f | 1 f | 4 f |
| Punch | 6 f | 2 f | 1 f | 6 f |
| Dash | 6–8 f | 3 f | 2 f | 8–10 f |
| Double take | 4 f | 2 f + hold 3 f | 2 f | 6 f |
| Big jump | 8 f | 4 f up, 4 f down | 2 f | 10 f |

## Rules of thumb

- Anticipation is 2–3× the length of the action it precedes.
- Never put a breakdown key in the fast frames; let the curve do the work.
- One frame of contact squash reads; three frames looks like slow motion.
- Settle amplitude should decay by roughly half per swing, over 2–3 swings.
